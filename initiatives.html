Housing System Accountability

I focus on identifying where publicly funded housing systems fail and translating those failures into structured findings that demand response.

Participant Protection

Ensuring that tenants are not just placed, but protected, stabilized, and treated with dignity within the systems designed to serve them.

Policy Translation

Bridging lived experience and regulatory language so that failures are not dismissed as isolated incidents but recognized as systemic gaps.

Public Tools

Developing accessible frameworks that allow others to understand, document, and navigate housing systems effectively.
