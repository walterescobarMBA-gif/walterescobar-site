Overview

This audit documents participant-level experience within a federally funded housing program under HOPWA administration. It identifies structural gaps, failures in transition planning, and inconsistencies in program execution.

Core Findings

Failure to provide structured transition planning at end of term.

Breakdown in communication and participant guidance.

Inconsistent application of policies and procedures.

Delayed or unclear responses to participant escalation.

Lack of transparency in administrative process.

Why This Matters

Housing programs are designed to stabilize vulnerable individuals. When process clarity breaks down, participants are exposed to unnecessary instability, confusion, and displacement risk.
