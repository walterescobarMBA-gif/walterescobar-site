My Story

I did not arrive at this work through theory. I arrived through direct experience with housing instability, institutional confusion, and systems that often speak in policy while people live the consequences.

My background gave me a rare lens: stability as a child, rigid regulated systems through credit union work, and firsthand experience navigating housing programs when stability was at stake.

That combination became the foundation for my work.

What This Work Teaches

A system’s failure is rarely one moment. It is usually a chain of unclear responsibility, weak communication, missing controls, and no meaningful correction.

My work focuses on identifying those chains. Not to make noise. To create clarity.

When housing programs are funded to stabilize vulnerable communities, accountability cannot be decorative. It has to operate in real time.

Purpose

My purpose is rooted in love for my community and a refusal to accept systems that destabilize the people they are funded to protect.

I believe lived experience becomes powerful when it is organized, documented, and translated into language institutions cannot easily dismiss.

Direction

I am building toward public policy, housing oversight, and systems accountability work that makes participant protection more than a promise.

This site is the platform where that work begins to take shape.

Core Thesis

Housing systems should stabilize people. When they create confusion, fear, or displacement pressure instead, the failure is not just personal. It is structural.
