My Story

I did not arrive at this work through theory. I arrived through direct experience navigating housing instability, institutional gaps, and systems that often speak in policy while people live the consequences.

My perspective was shaped by navigating different systems with very different expectations. That experience made one thing clear: how a system is designed and how it actually operates are often not the same.

What I experienced was not random. The same breakdowns appeared again and again: unclear communication, shifting responsibility, and a lack of meaningful accountability when stability was on the line.

Over time, I stopped viewing those moments as isolated incidents. I began documenting them, organizing them, and identifying the patterns behind them.

That shift, from experiencing the system to analyzing it, became the foundation of my work.

What This Work Teaches

A system’s failure is rarely one moment. It is usually a chain of unclear responsibility, weak communication, missing controls, and no meaningful correction.

My work focuses on identifying those chains. Not to make noise. To create clarity.
