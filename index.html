Housing Systems Accountability Project

A participant-perspective chronology and systems-analysis project examining administrative fragmentation, procedural instability, oversight escalation, and housing-transition workflows within a layered stabilization ecosystem.

Project Overview

This project began as an effort to preserve records during a period of housing instability and administrative uncertainty.

Over time, the accumulation of municipal correspondence, oversight activity, participant communications, procedural notices, and chronology reconstruction evolved into a broader systems-oriented analysis.

The archive examines how layered administrative systems are experienced, navigated, and documented from the participant perspective in real time.
