Purpose

This page organizes key evidence into a readable record. The goal is not to overwhelm the reader with documents, but to connect claims, supporting records, and relevance in a clear structure.

Evidence 1: Sponsor Acknowledgment of Uncoordinated Relocations

Claim

Participant relocations occurred without direct coordination through APLA Health program staff.

Evidence

APLA Health acknowledged that HSS initiated certain participant relocations and that those activities were not directly coordinated through APLA Health program staff when they occurred.

Relevance

Supports the concern that sponsor-level oversight and coordination controls were not functioning during participant relocation events.

Impact: Demonstrates breakdown between sponsor responsibility and subcontractor action.

Evidence 2: July 16 Letter Later Characterized as Non-Disciplinary

Claim

Program communication created housing-related pressure and uncertainty.

Evidence

APLA Health later stated that the July 16, 2025 letter was part of a standard compliance process and was not disciplinary.

Relevance

This creates a documented contrast between the sponsor’s later explanation and the participant-facing impact of language referencing future housing opportunities.

Impact: Supports review of whether participant-facing communications were clear, accurate, and non-coercive.

Evidence 3: Flexible Program Limit Presented as Fixed

Claim

Program limits were treated as rigid despite later clarification that flexibility existed.

Evidence

LAHD guidance later clarified that the 24-month limit remained a best practice, not a strict regulatory requirement.

Relevance

Supports the concern that unwritten or overstated rules were used in ways affecting housing stability.

Impact: Shows why written policy authority and clear participant guidance matter.

Evidence 4: Reasonable Accommodation Denied, Then Approved

Claim

A requested accommodation to complete TBRA-related steps electronically was initially denied despite feasibility.

Evidence

The request was denied at the program level and later approved by supervisory staff, confirming that the requested process was possible.

Relevance

Supports review of whether the accommodation process was properly evaluated and whether an interactive process occurred.

Impact: Demonstrates unnecessary administrative resistance during a critical housing transition.

Evidence 5: Contradictory TBRA Communication

Claim

TBRA assistance was represented as unavailable and later offered without clear explanation.

Evidence

The audit timeline identifies TBRA communication changing from unavailable to offered between late October and mid-November 2025.

Relevance

Supports the concern that program decision-making and communication were inconsistent during transition planning.

Impact: Contradictory communication increased uncertainty and delayed stabilization.

Note on Evidence

Evidence may include emails, notices, letters, public records, participant statements, and audit exhibits. Additional documents may be added as the record develops.
