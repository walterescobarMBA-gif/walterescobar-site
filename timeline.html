Overview

This timeline presents the sequence of events following the end of a HOPWA transitional housing term. It is designed to show how lack of planning, inconsistent communication, and delayed response escalated housing instability.

June 2, 2025

Program term ended.

Failure: No documented exit plan, transition pathway, or follow-up support provided.

Impact: Participant was left to navigate a housing transition without structured guidance.

June 2025

Required documentation was submitted and participant engaged available housing support channels.

Failure: No clear next steps or coordinated transition support followed.

Impact: Administrative burden shifted onto the participant.

July 3, 2025

Same-day vacate / zero-hour displacement notice issued.

Failure: Displacement pressure occurred without prior stabilization planning.

Impact: Immediate housing instability risk triggered.

July 16, 2025

Compliance / exit letter issued using unclear or unsupported policy framing.

Failure: Unclear standards were applied as housing-impacting rules.

Impact: Participant faced increased pressure without a transparent policy basis.

August 2025

Oversight later determined participant should not be exited.

Failure: Prior actions were inconsistent with later oversight determination.

Impact: Demonstrated premature or unsupported program action.

September 27, 2025

First 3-Day Pay-or-Quit notice issued.

Failure: Eviction-risk event occurred without documented stabilization response.

Impact: Displacement risk escalated.

October 31 – November 12, 2025

TBRA assistance was represented as unavailable, then later offered.

Failure: Contradictory program communication created avoidable uncertainty.

Impact: Transition planning was delayed and destabilized.

November 2025

Reasonable accommodation request to complete TBRA process electronically was denied, then later approved by supervisory staff.

Failure: Accommodation process broke down despite feasibility.

Impact: Participant was forced to overcome unnecessary administrative resistance.

December 2025

Second 3-Day Pay-or-Quit notice issued asserting a significant balance.

Failure: No effective stabilization response documented during active eviction-risk period.

Impact: Housing insecurity continued despite repeated notice and escalation.

Conclusion

This sequence reflects repeated failures at key transition points: no documented pathway, unclear rules, contradictory communication, accommodation resistance, and eviction-risk escalation.
